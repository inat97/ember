Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026

The short version. Ember does not collect your data. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. Everything you log is stored on your iPhone and never leaves it, except when you deliberately share or print a summary yourself.

1. Who this policy is from

Ember is developed by Nguyen Anh Tuan ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the Ember iPhone app. Contact: 1551010058.linh+ember@gmail.com

2. What Ember stores, and where

Ember stores the entries you create: symptom ratings, hot flash counts, sleep, mood, period flow, free-text notes, and any treatments you choose to record, along with your app settings and reminder preferences.

All of it is written to your device's local storage. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else. We have no database, no server, and no way to read it.

3. What we do not collect

  • No account, email address, name, or profile.
  • No analytics, usage tracking, or event logging.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No location, contacts, photos, or health records from other apps.

The app functions with your device offline.

4. On-device intelligence

On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, Ember may ask a language model running on the device itself to suggest one question you might raise at an appointment. The request and its answer stay on the device. No summary, entry, or note is sent to us, to Apple, or to any other service for this feature.

All figures in your appointment summary are calculated by the app from your own entries. The model is not asked to produce numbers.

5. Notifications

If you turn on the evening reminder, Ember schedules local notifications on your device. No push server is involved and no notification content leaves your iPhone.

6. Subscriptions and Apple

Ember is sold as a subscription through Apple. Payment, billing, renewals and cancellations are handled entirely by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never see your payment details.

Apple provides us with aggregated, non-identifying sales and subscription reports. If you separately choose to share App Analytics or crash diagnostics with developers in your iOS settings, Apple may include our app in that aggregated data. Neither contains the contents of your symptom log.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Your entries stay on your device until you delete them. Deleting the Ember app removes its data from that device.

Because there is no server, we cannot restore anything for you. If your device is lost or reset, the only copy is whatever exists in your own device backup. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, your Ember data is included in that backup and is governed by the terms of that backup service.

8. Children

Ember is not directed to children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children — in fact, we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

9. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export, or delete personal data held about you. We hold none: there is no copy of your information in our possession to access, export, or erase. The data lives on your device and is under your control there. We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not have any.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at https://inat97.github.io/ember/privacy.html with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
