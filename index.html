Ember

A perimenopause symptom diary for iPhone.

Ember is a place to record hot flashes, night sweats, sleep, mood and the rest of it, one evening at a time — and to turn months of those entries into a single page you can hand to your doctor.

Everything stays on your iPhone. Ember has no server and no account.

Ember is not a medical device

Ember records what you tell it and describes it back. It does not diagnose, does not interpret symptoms, and never suggests a medication or a dose. Decisions about your health belong with you and your clinician.
